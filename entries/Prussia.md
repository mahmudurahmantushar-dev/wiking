# Prussia

## 📖 Introduction
**Prussia** (German: *Preußen*) was a European state that originated in the Duchy of Prussia (1525) and later expanded into a powerful kingdom.  
Its capital shifted from **Königsberg** to **Berlin**, and it became the driving force behind German unification.

## 🕰️ History / Timeline
- **1525**: Duchy of Prussia established as a Polish fief.  
- **1701**: Kingdom of Prussia proclaimed under Frederick I.  
- **1740–1786**: Reign of Frederick the Great; Prussia becomes a leading military power.  
- **1815**: Congress of Vienna confirms Prussia as a major European state.  
- **1871**: Prussia leads German unification; Berlin becomes capital of the German Empire.  
- **1918**: Monarchy abolished after World War I; Prussia becomes a Free State.  
- **1947**: Officially dissolved by Allied powers after World War II.  

## 🔑 Key Features
- ⚔️ **Military Tradition**: Known for discipline and efficiency, symbolized by Frederick the Great.  
- 🏛️ **Government**: Absolute monarchy evolving into constitutional structures.  
- 🌍 **Expansion**: Controlled territories across modern Germany, Poland, Lithuania, and beyond.  
- 📚 **Culture**: Influenced philosophy (Kant, Hegel), science, and education.  
- 🏗️ **Role in Germany**: Dominated the German Empire until 1918.  

## 🌍 Legacy / Impact
- Prussia’s military and bureaucratic traditions shaped modern Germany.  
- Its dissolution marked the end of centuries of militaristic dominance.  
- Still remembered as a symbol of discipline, order, and state power.  

## 🌟 Fun Fact
The motto of Prussia was **“Gott mit uns”** (“God with us”), later adopted by the German Empire.
