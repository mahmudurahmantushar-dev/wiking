# 🚀 S‑400 Triumf (SA‑21 Growler)

## 📖 Introduction
The **S‑400 Triumf** is Russia’s most advanced long‑range surface‑to‑air missile system.  
It can engage aircraft, drones, cruise missiles, and ballistic missiles at ranges up to **400 km** and altitudes of **30 km**.

## 🕰️ History / Origins
- Developed in the **1990s** by **Almaz‑Antey** as a successor to the S‑300.  
- Entered service in **2007** with the Russian military.  
- Exported to countries including **China, Turkey, and India**.  

## 🔑 Key Features
- 🎯 **Range**: Up to **400 km** with the 40N6E missile.  
- 🛡️ **Altitude Coverage**: Up to **30 km**.  
- 🚀 **Missiles**: Multiple types (40N6E, 48N6, 9M96) for layered defense.  
- 📡 **Radar**: Advanced phased‑array radars track hundreds of targets simultaneously.  
- 🏗️ **Mobility**: Truck‑mounted launchers for rapid deployment.  
- 🌐 **Capacity**: Can engage up to **80 targets** with **160 missiles** at once.  

## 🌍 Legacy / Impact
- Considered one of the **most advanced air defense systems globally**.  
- Deployed in conflicts such as the **Syrian Civil War** and **Russo‑Ukrainian War**.  
- India’s deployment (“Sudarshan”) strengthens its regional air defense.  
- Turkey’s purchase caused tensions with NATO and U.S. sanctions.  

## 🌟 Fun Fact
The S‑400 can detect stealth aircraft at long ranges, making it a major rival to Western systems like the **Patriot** and **THAAD**.
