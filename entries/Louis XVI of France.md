# Louis XVI of France

## 📖 Introduction
**Louis XVI**, born **Louis-Auguste**, was King of France from 1774 until 1792.  
His reign saw the collapse of the Ancien Régime and the outbreak of the French Revolution.

## 🕰️ Life & Timeline
- **Born**: 23 August 1754, Palace of Versailles.  
- **1770**: Married Marie Antoinette of Austria.  
- **1774**: Became King of France at age 20.  
- **1778–1783**: Supported American Revolution against Britain, worsening France’s debt.  
- **1789**: Estates-General convened; French Revolution begins.  
- **1791**: Attempted escape in the *Flight to Varennes*, captured and returned to Paris.  
- **1792**: Monarchy abolished; Louis declared “Citizen Capet.”  
- **1793**: Tried and executed by guillotine on 21 January.  

## 🔑 Key Facts
- 💰 **Financial Crisis**: France’s debt and taxation system fueled unrest.  
- 📜 **Revolution**: His indecision and resistance to reform deepened revolutionary fervor.  
- ⚖️ **Trial**: Convicted of treason by the National Convention.  
- 🏛️ **Family**: Father of Louis XVII (the “Lost Dauphin”).  

## 🌍 Legacy / Impact
- His downfall symbolized the end of **absolute monarchy** in France.  
- His execution shocked Europe and accelerated revolutionary change.  
- Remembered as a tragic figure caught between tradition and reform.  

## 🌟 Fun Fact
Louis XVI was fascinated by **locksmithing** and often worked on locks at Versailles as a hobby.
