# 🛡️ Rafael Advanced Defense Systems

## 📖 Introduction
**Rafael Advanced Defense Systems Ltd.** is Israel’s leading government‑owned defense technology company.  
It specializes in **air defense, missile systems, electronic warfare, and cyber defense**, providing combat‑proven solutions worldwide.

## 🕰️ History / Origins
- **Founded**: 1948 as part of Israel’s **Science Corps**.  
- **2002**: Became a government‑owned corporation.  
- **Headquarters**: Kiryat Bialik, Israel.  
- **CEO**: Yoav Har‑Even.  

## 🔑 Key Features
- 🎯 **Products**:  
  - **Iron Dome** – short‑range rocket defense.  
  - **David’s Sling** – medium‑range missile defense.  
  - **Trophy APS** – active protection system for tanks.  
  - **Spike missile family** – precision‑guided weapons.  
- 🌐 **Global Reach**: Supplies systems to NATO, the U.S., India, and other allies.  
- 💰 **Revenue**: ~$4.85 billion USD (2024).  
- 👥 **Employees**: ~10,000 worldwide.  

## 🌍 Legacy / Impact
- Rafael’s technologies have **reshaped modern warfare**, especially missile defense.  
- Its systems are deployed in **Israel, Europe, Asia, and the U.S.**, strengthening global security partnerships.  
- Known for **innovation in AI, cyber defense, and quantum research**.  

## 🌟 Fun Fact
The name **“Rafael”** is an acronym for *“Rikuz Peilut Lehitnahalut”* (Authority for Weapons Development), symbolizing its roots in Israel’s early defense efforts.
