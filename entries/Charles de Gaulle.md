# Charles de Gaulle

## 📖 Introduction
**Charles André Joseph Marie de Gaulle** was a French military leader and statesman.  
He became the symbol of French resistance during World War II and later reshaped France’s political system.

## 🕰️ Life & Career
- **Born**: 22 November 1890, Lille, France.  
- **Military Service**: Served in World War I, captured at Verdun (1916).  
- **World War II**:  
  - Refused to accept France’s surrender in 1940.  
  - Led the **Free French Forces** from London.  
  - Delivered the famous *Appeal of 18 June* urging resistance.  
- **Post‑war**: Headed the Provisional Government (1944–1946).  
- **President**: Founded the **Fifth Republic** in 1958, serving as President until 1969.  
- **Death**: 9 November 1970, Colombey‑les‑Deux‑Églises.  

## 🔑 Achievements
- 🛡️ **WWII Leadership**: Unified French resistance against Nazi occupation.  
- 🏛️ **Fifth Republic**: Created a strong presidential system still in place today.  
- 🌍 **Foreign Policy**: Asserted French independence from NATO’s command, pursued nuclear deterrence.  
- 🇩🇿 **Decolonization**: Oversaw Algerian independence (1962).  
- 🎤 **Oratory**: Famous for speeches that inspired national pride and resilience.  

## 🌍 Legacy / Impact
- Seen as the **savior of French honor** during WWII.  
- His reforms stabilized French politics after years of instability.  
- Remembered as both a **nationalist** and a **visionary modernizer**.  

## 🌟 Fun Fact
Paris’s main airport, **Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG)**, is named in his honor.
