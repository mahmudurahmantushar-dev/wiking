# Olympic Games

## 📖 Introduction
The **Olympic Games** are a global multi‑sport event organized by the **International Olympic Committee (IOC)**.  
They include the **Summer Olympics** and **Winter Olympics**, alternating every two years within the four‑year cycle.

## 🕰️ History
- **Ancient Olympics**: First held in Olympia, Greece (776 BCE).  
- **Modern Olympics**: Revived in 1896 by Pierre de Coubertin in Athens.  
- **Winter Olympics**: First held in Chamonix, France (1924).  

## 🔑 Key Features
- 🌍 **Global Participation**: Over 200 nations compete.  
- 🏛️ **Headquarters**: Lausanne, Switzerland.  
- 🎯 **Motto**: *Citius, Altius, Fortius – Communiter* (“Faster, Higher, Stronger – Together”).  
- 🏆 **Events**: Athletics, swimming, gymnastics, football, basketball, and more.  
- 📺 **Cultural Impact**: Symbolized by the Olympic rings, torch relay, and opening ceremonies.  

## 🌍 Upcoming Games
- **Paris 2024 (Summer)**: July 26 – August 11, 2024.  
- **Milano‑Cortina 2026 (Winter)**: February 6 – 22, 2026.  
- **Los Angeles 2028 (Summer)**: July 14 – 30, 2028.  
- **Brisbane 2032 (Summer)**: July 23 – August 8, 2032.  

## 🌟 Fun Fact
The Olympic flame is lit in Olympia, Greece, and carried to the host city via a **torch relay**.
