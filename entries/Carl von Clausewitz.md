# Carl von Clausewitz

## 📖 Introduction
**Carl Philipp Gottlieb von Clausewitz** was a Prussian general, strategist, and military theorist.  
His ideas remain central to modern military strategy, political science, and international relations.

## 🕰️ Life & Career
- **Born**: 1 July 1780, Burg bei Magdeburg, Prussia.  
- **Military Service**: Joined the Prussian Army at age 12; fought in the Napoleonic Wars.  
- **Key Battles**: Jena–Auerstedt (1806), Borodino (1812), Ligny and Wavre (1815).  
- **Academic Role**: Directed the Prussian War Academy (*Kriegsakademie*).  
- **Died**: 16 November 1831, Breslau, Prussia.  

## 🔑 Key Ideas
- ⚔️ **War as Politics**: War is not autonomous but tied to political objectives.  
- 🧠 **Fog of War**: Uncertainty and chance dominate the battlefield.  
- 💡 **Center of Gravity**: Identify and strike the enemy’s main source of strength.  
- 🔄 **Trinity of War**: War involves a dynamic balance between government, military, and people.  
- 📚 **On War (Vom Kriege)**: His unfinished masterpiece, published posthumously by his wife Marie von Brühl.  

## 🌍 Legacy / Impact
- Influenced military leaders from Moltke to Eisenhower.  
- His theories shaped modern doctrines of strategy, deterrence, and geopolitics.  
- Still studied in military academies worldwide.  

## 🌟 Fun Fact
Clausewitz briefly served in the **Russian Army** during Napoleon’s invasion of 1812, fighting at Borodino.
