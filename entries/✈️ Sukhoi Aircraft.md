# ✈️ Sukhoi Aircraft

## 📖 Introduction
**Sukhoi** is a major Russian aircraft manufacturer, founded in 1939 by **Pavel Sukhoi**.  
It is best known for producing advanced **fighter jets, bombers, and civilian aircraft**, many of which form the backbone of the Russian Air Force and are exported worldwide.

## 🕰️ History
- **1939**: Sukhoi Design Bureau established in Moscow.  
- **Cold War Era**: Produced fighters like the Su‑7, Su‑15, and Su‑24.  
- **1980s**: Introduction of the **Su‑27 Flanker**, a fourth‑generation air superiority fighter.  
- **2000s–Present**: Development of multirole fighters (Su‑30, Su‑35) and the fifth‑generation stealth **Su‑57**.  
- Civil aviation branch created the **Sukhoi Superjet 100**, a regional passenger aircraft.

## 🔑 Famous Models
- **Su‑27 “Flanker”**: Highly maneuverable air superiority fighter.  
- **Su‑30**: Multirole fighter, widely exported.  
- **Su‑35**: Advanced version of Su‑27 with thrust‑vectoring engines.  
- **Su‑57**: Fifth‑generation stealth fighter, Russia’s answer to the U.S. F‑22 and F‑35.  
- **Su‑25 “Frogfoot”**: Close air support jet, similar to the U.S. A‑10.  
- **Sukhoi Superjet 100**: Civilian regional jet.

## 🌍 Importance
- Central to Russia’s **air defense and strike capabilities**.  
- Exported to countries including India, China, Vietnam, and Algeria.  
- Known for **supermaneuverability**, heavy payloads, and advanced avionics.  

## ⚠️ Challenges
- High maintenance costs compared to Western jets.  
- Civilian Superjet faced reliability issues in early years.  
- Export versions often differ in capability from domestic models.

## 🌟 Fun Fact
The **Su‑27 Flanker** can reach speeds over **Mach 2.3**, making it one of the fastest fighter jets ever built.
