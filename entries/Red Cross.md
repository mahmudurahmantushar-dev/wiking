# Red Cross

## 📖 Introduction
The **Red Cross** is a humanitarian organization dedicated to protecting human life and dignity.  
It operates under the principles of **neutrality, impartiality, and independence**, offering aid in wars, disasters, and health crises.

## 🕰️ History / Origins
- **Founded**: 1863 in Geneva, Switzerland.  
- **Founders**: Henry Dunant, Gustave Moynier, Théodore Maunoir, Guillaume Henri Dufour, Louis Appia.  
- **Symbols**: Red Cross, Red Crescent, and Red Crystal.  
- **Branches**:  
  - **ICRC (International Committee of the Red Cross)** – humanitarian law and conflict zones.  
  - **IFRC (International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies)** – disaster relief and health.  
  - **National Societies** – e.g., American Red Cross, Bangladesh Red Crescent.

## 🔑 Key Features
- 🚑 **Emergency Relief**: Disaster response, refugee aid, and medical support.  
- 🩺 **Health Services**: Blood donations, first aid training, vaccination campaigns.  
- ⚖️ **Humanitarian Law**: Protects civilians and prisoners of war under the Geneva Conventions.  
- 🌍 **Global Reach**: Active in nearly every country, with millions of volunteers.  

## 🌍 Legacy / Impact
- Saved countless lives in wars, earthquakes, floods, and pandemics.  
- Symbol of **neutral humanitarian aid** recognized worldwide.  
- Partnered with governments and NGOs to strengthen disaster preparedness.  

## 🌟 Fun Fact
The **Red Cross emblem** is the reverse of the Swiss flag, honoring Switzerland’s neutrality.
