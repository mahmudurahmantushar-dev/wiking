# Arab Spring

## 📖 Introduction
The **Arab Spring** was a series of popular uprisings and protests across the Arab world.  
It began in **Tunisia (December 2010)** and quickly spread to **Egypt, Libya, Syria, Yemen, Bahrain, and other nations**.  
The movement was driven by demands for **democracy, economic opportunity, and an end to authoritarian rule**.

## 🕰️ Timeline
- **Dec 2010**: Mohamed Bouazizi’s self‑immolation in Tunisia sparks protests.  
- **Jan 2011**: Tunisian President Zine al‑Abidine Ben Ali flees.  
- **Feb 2011**: Egyptian President Hosni Mubarak resigns after mass protests in Tahrir Square.  
- **2011**: Civil wars erupt in Libya and Syria; Muammar Gaddafi is overthrown in Libya.  
- **2011–2012**: Yemen’s Ali Abdullah Saleh steps down; Bahrain sees suppressed protests.  

## 🔑 Causes
- 📉 **Economic hardship**: unemployment, inflation, poverty.  
- ⚖️ **Political repression**: authoritarian regimes, corruption, lack of freedoms.  
- 🌐 **Social media activism**: Facebook, Twitter, and YouTube amplified voices and organized protests.  
- 🔥 **Trigger**: Bouazizi’s protest against police harassment in Tunisia.  

## 🌍 Legacy / Impact
- Tunisia transitioned to democracy (though fragile today).  
- Egypt saw a brief democratic opening, later followed by military rule.  
- Libya and Syria descended into prolonged civil wars.  
- The Arab Spring reshaped global debates on **democracy, human rights, and digital activism**.  

## 🌟 Fun Fact
The term **“Arab Spring”** was inspired by the **“Prague Spring”** of 1968, symbolizing hope for freedom.
