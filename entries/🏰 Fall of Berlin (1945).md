# 🏰 Fall of Berlin (1945)

## 📖 Introduction
The **Fall of Berlin** was the final major offensive of the **European theater of World War II**.  
It took place in **April–May 1945**, when Soviet forces captured Berlin, leading to the unconditional surrender of Nazi Germany.

## 🕰️ Timeline
- **16 April 1945**: Soviet Union launches the **Battle of Berlin**.  
- **20 April 1945**: Soviet artillery begins bombardment of Berlin on Adolf Hitler’s birthday.  
- **25 April 1945**: Soviet and American forces meet at the Elbe River, cutting Germany in two.  
- **30 April 1945**: Adolf Hitler commits suicide in his bunker.  
- **2 May 1945**: Berlin officially surrenders to Soviet forces.  
- **8 May 1945**: Germany signs unconditional surrender; **Victory in Europe (VE) Day**.

## 🔑 Causes
- Continuous Allied advances from the west and Soviet offensives from the east.  
- Collapse of German military strength after years of attrition.  
- Strategic importance of Berlin as the Nazi capital.

## 🌍 Consequences
- **End of World War II in Europe**: Germany surrendered unconditionally.  
- **Division of Berlin**: City split into four occupation zones (Soviet, American, British, French).  
- **Cold War Symbolism**: Berlin later became a focal point of East–West tensions, leading to the Berlin Wall.  
- **Casualties**: Estimated 80,000 Soviet soldiers killed; hundreds of thousands of German soldiers and civilians dead.

## ⚖️ Strategic Significance
- Demonstrated Soviet military dominance in Eastern Europe.  
- Marked the collapse of Nazi Germany’s leadership and ideology.  
- Set the stage for postwar geopolitics and the **division of Germany**.

## 🌟 Fun Fact
The **Reichstag building** was heavily damaged during the battle, and Soviet soldiers famously raised the **Red Flag** over it on 2 May 1945 — a symbol of Nazi defeat.
