# Clement Attlee

## 📖 Introduction
**Clement Richard Attlee, 1st Earl Attlee** was a British statesman and leader of the Labour Party.  
He served as Prime Minister from **1945 to 1951**, overseeing major social and economic reforms that reshaped post‑war Britain.

## 🕰️ Life & Career
- **Born**: 3 January 1883, Putney, London.  
- **Early Career**: Lawyer, social worker, and lecturer before entering politics.  
- **Political Rise**: Became Labour MP in 1922; Deputy Prime Minister in Churchill’s wartime coalition (1940–1945).  
- **Prime Minister**: 1945–1951, leading Britain through post‑war reconstruction.  
- **Death**: 8 October 1967, London.  

## 🔑 Achievements
- 🏥 **Founded the NHS (1948)** – free healthcare for all citizens.  
- 🏛️ **Welfare State** – expanded social security, housing, and education.  
- ⚙️ **Nationalization** – coal, steel, railways, and utilities brought under state control.  
- 🌍 **Foreign Policy** – oversaw decolonization, including independence for India (1947).  
- 🛡️ **Cold War** – supported NATO and Britain’s nuclear program.  

## 🌍 Legacy / Impact
- Remembered as one of the most **effective reforming Prime Ministers**.  
- His government laid the foundation for modern British social democracy.  
- Ranked highly in historical assessments of UK leaders.  

## 🌟 Fun Fact
Attlee was known for his **quiet, modest personality**, famously described by Churchill as *“a modest man with much to be modest about”* — yet his achievements were monumental.
