# New Testament

## 📖 Introduction
The **New Testament (NT)** is the second division of the Christian Bible.  
It focuses on the life, teachings, death, and resurrection of **Jesus Christ**, and the early Christian Church.  
Written in **Greek** between ~50 CE and 100 CE, it is foundational to Christian theology and worship.

## 🕰️ History / Origins
- **Language**: Koine Greek.  
- **Composition**: Written by apostles and early disciples.  
- **Canon Differences**:  
  - **Protestant, Catholic, Orthodox**: All include **27 books**.  
- **Themes**: Salvation through Christ, spreading the Gospel, guidance for Christian living.

## 🔑 Key Features
- ✝️ **Focus on Jesus**: His ministry, crucifixion, and resurrection.  
- 🌍 **Early Church**: Growth and struggles of the first Christian communities.  
- 📖 **Letters**: Guidance on faith, morality, and church leadership.  
- 🔮 **Prophecy**: Revelation describes end‑times and hope for believers.  

## 🌍 Legacy / Impact
- Central to **Christian doctrine and worship**.  
- Influenced **law, ethics, literature, and art** worldwide.  
- Basis for countless translations, commentaries, and theological debates.  

## 🌟 Fun Fact
The shortest verse in the New Testament is **John 11:35 — “Jesus wept.”**
