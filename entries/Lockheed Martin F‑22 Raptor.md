# Lockheed Martin F‑22 Raptor

## 📖 Introduction
The **F‑22 Raptor** is a twin‑engine, supersonic, all‑weather stealth fighter aircraft.  
It was developed under the **Advanced Tactical Fighter (ATF)** program to ensure U.S. air dominance in the 21st century.

## 🕰️ History
- **First flight**: 7 September 1997.  
- **Introduced**: 15 December 2005.  
- **Production**: 1996–2011; 195 built (187 operational, 8 test aircraft).  
- **Primary user**: United States Air Force.

## 🔑 Capabilities
- **Stealth**: Radar‑evading design with internal weapons bays.  
- **Speed**: Mach 2+ with supercruise (supersonic flight without afterburners).  
- **Agility**: Thrust‑vectoring nozzles for unmatched maneuverability.  
- **Avionics**: Advanced sensors, electronic warfare, and data‑fusion systems.  
- **Role**: Air superiority, ground attack, electronic warfare, and signals intelligence.

## 🌍 Strategic Importance
- Ensures U.S. air dominance against advanced threats.  
- Serves as a deterrent in contested airspaces.  
- Influenced the design of later stealth aircraft like the **F‑35 Lightning II**.

## ⚖️ Legacy
- Considered the world’s first true **fifth‑generation fighter**.  
- Despite limited production, it remains a cornerstone of U.S. air power.  
- Its unmatched kill ratio in exercises (reportedly 108:0 against legacy fighters) highlights its dominance.

## 🌟 Fun Fact
The F‑22 was so advanced that U.S. law prohibited its export, making it exclusive to the Air Force.
