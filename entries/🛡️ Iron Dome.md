# 🛡️ Iron Dome

## 📖 Introduction
The **Iron Dome** is Israel’s mobile air defense system, designed to intercept and destroy short‑range rockets, artillery shells, and mortars.  
It has been operational since **2011** and is considered one of the most effective missile defense systems in the world.

## 🕰️ History / Origins
- **2005**: Developed by **Rafael Advanced Defense Systems** and **Israel Aerospace Industries**.  
- **2011**: First deployed by the Israel Defense Forces (IDF).  
- Created in response to frequent rocket attacks from Gaza and southern Lebanon.  

## 🔑 Key Features
- 🚀 **Tamir Interceptor Missiles**: Lightweight, radar‑guided missiles designed to destroy incoming threats mid‑air.  
- 📡 **Radar & Control System**: Detects launches, predicts impact points, and decides whether interception is necessary.  
- 💰 **Cost**: Each battery costs about **$50 million**; each interception costs **$100,000–150,000**.  
- ⚡ **Deployment**: Around 10 batteries currently in service, with plans for expansion.  
- 🌐 **Success Rate**: Over **90% effectiveness**, with thousands of rockets intercepted.  

## 🌍 Legacy / Impact
- Has saved thousands of lives by protecting Israeli cities during conflicts.  
- Widely praised as one of the most advanced missile defense systems globally.  
- Tested and partially adopted by other countries, including the **United States Army**.  

## 🌟 Fun Fact
The Iron Dome doesn’t intercept every rocket — it only targets those predicted to hit populated areas, saving resources while maximizing protection.
